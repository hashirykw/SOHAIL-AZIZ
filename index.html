        Questions students ask

        Which A Level syllabus does Sir Suhail follow?

        Cambridge International AS & A Level Chemistry (9701), the syllabus for exams in 2025, 2026 and 2027. Cambridge has published the 2028–2030 version and lists no significant changes that affect teaching, so the topics here carry over.

        Does he teach O Level and IGCSE as well?

        Yes. Alongside A Level (9701), Sir Suhail teaches O Level Chemistry (5070) and IGCSE Chemistry (0620).

        Are the classes online or in person?

        Both. There are physical and evening classes, and interactive live sessions online on Zoom. Recorded lectures are available 24/7, and you can send questions on WhatsApp.

        What’s the difference between AS and A Level?

        AS covers topics 1–22 and practical skills, assessed in Papers 1, 2 and 3. The full A Level adds topics 23–37, assessed in Paper 4, plus Paper 5 on planning, analysis and evaluation. Paper 4 assumes everything from AS.

        Can I take AS first and finish A Level the next year?

        Yes. Cambridge allows a staged route: sit AS (Papers 1–3) in one series, then Papers 4 and 5 later, carrying the AS result forward under its rules and time limits.

        Is there a practical exam?

        Paper 3 is a two-hour lab practical worth 40 marks, taken for AS and for A Level. Paper 5 is a 1 hour 15 minute written paper on planning, analysis and evaluation, worth 30 marks, for A Level only.
