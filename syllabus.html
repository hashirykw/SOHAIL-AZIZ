Physical Inorganic Organic Analysis Practical

The five papers

AS Level is Papers 1, 2 and 3. The full A Level adds Papers 4 and 5. AS is graded a–e; A Level routes are graded A*–E.

PaperWhat it isTimeMarksAS weightA Level weight

Practical skills

Paper 3 is a timetabled lab practical. Paper 5 is a written paper on planning, analysis and evaluation, and its questions can use chemistry outside the syllabus content.

  • 2 h · 40 marksPaper 3: two or three questions. One is an observational problem on unknown substances; the others are quantitative, such as a titration.
  • 0.05 cm3Record burette readings to the nearest 0.05 cm3.
  • 0.10 cm3Concordant titres are two within 0.10 cm3 of each other.
  • 0.5 °CWith a thermometer marked in 1 °C steps, read to the nearest 0.5 °C.
  • 1 h 15 · 30 marksPaper 5, A Level only: plan an experiment, analyse data, evaluate the method.
  • 20%+Cambridge recommends spending at least a fifth of course time on hands-on practical work.
