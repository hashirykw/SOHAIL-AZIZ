A periodic table, electronic configurations, an Mr calculator and the mole calculations that come up in every paper. Each one shows its working, so you can check yours.

Periodic table

Tap an element for its details. Relative atomic masses are given to one decimal place, the precision 9701 papers use. Filter by type, or search by name, symbol or atomic number.

Scroll the table sideways to see every group.

Electronic configuration

Full and shorthand configurations with electrons in boxes, for atoms and ions from hydrogen to krypton — the range the syllabus assesses.

Mr calculator

Type any formula. Brackets, square brackets and hydrates (use a dot: CuSO4.5H2O) all work. You get the relative formula mass and the percentage by mass of each element.

Capitals matter: Co is cobalt, CO is carbon monoxide.

Mole calculators

Fill in every box except the one you want, and the answer appears with its working. Numbers like 1.74e-5 work for powers of ten.
